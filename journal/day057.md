## Professor Questions & Answers

### Q1. Why revise difficult concepts?

**Answer:** Revision provides additional practice and helps correct misunderstandings before moving to more advanced material.

### Q2. What is better than memorizing a command?

**Answer:** Understanding the purpose of the command and knowing when it should be used.

### Q3. What should you do when a command gives unexpected output?

**Answer:** Inspect the output, check the syntax and assumptions, read the error message if present, and troubleshoot systematically.

### Q4. Why is practice important in cybersecurity learning?

**Answer:** Cybersecurity requires applying concepts to unfamiliar situations, so practical repetition helps develop problem-solving ability.
